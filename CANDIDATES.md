# Dream vacation emoji candidates (draft pass)

Exactly one emoji each; judged on the most evocative.

1. 🏝️ desert island -- the postcard dream: one palm, no
   meetings, unreachable by standup.
2. 🛌 bed -- the honest dream: not a place, a state; the
   vacation most adults are actually picturing. It wins on
   evocation because every reader feels it in their spine:
   the dream is not travel, it is unconsciousness without
   an alarm.
3. 🚀 rocket -- the maximalist dream: leave the planet,
   forward all mail to orbit.
4. 🧳 suitcase -- the journey itself, packed and ready.
5. 🏕️ camping -- the affordable dream: a tent, a fire, and
   weather doing whatever it wants.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2, 🛌. The island (candidate 1) is the
dream vacation as advertised; the bed is the dream vacation
as dreamed -- by adults, at 11 p.m., setting the alarm for
the dream's opposite. Evocativeness is the judging word,
and no glyph in the set lands in the reader's body faster:
you do not look at 🛌, you feel your shoulders drop.
